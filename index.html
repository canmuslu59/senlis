About Eazilo

Independent by design.

Eazilo is a product and development brand operated by founder Can Muslu in Sakarya, Türkiye. The founder's registered business is under Can Muslu; Eazilo identifies the software product portfolio rather than claiming to be a separately incorporated legal entity.

Founder-led development

Our work builds on hands-on experience in fragrance manufacturing and e-commerce, combined with iterative development of mobile apps, operational software and data-intensive products.

We are currently bootstrapped, without outside venture funding.

Product status and transparency

Some projects are production-oriented tools; others remain in testing, development or prototype stages. We disclose those distinctions and do not represent planned AI features as launched integrations.
