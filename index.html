Company & founder

Built by an independent developer.

Eazilo is the product and software development brand of Can Muslu, based in Sakarya, Türkiye. We develop software informed by hands-on experience in e-commerce, fragrance manufacturing and consumer-facing applications. Our Hisse Portföyüm Android application has been published on Google Play.

Eazilo is currently founder-funded and has not raised outside investment. We distinguish the Eazilo product brand from the founder's registered business identity for verification purposes.
