Flagship product · SENLIS

Find the fragrance you mean.

SENLIS is an AI-powered fragrance discovery and intelligence platform built around a large structured catalog of perfumes, body mists, fragrance notes, market data and product relationships.

SENLIS data foundation

AI is only as useful as the data underneath it.

SENLIS is being developed on top of a cleaned and normalized fragrance catalog designed for semantic search, recommendation and commercial enrichment.
