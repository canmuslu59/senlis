The platform

Discovery should start with intent, not a product name.

Most fragrance search experiences assume users already know what to type. SENLIS is being built to understand what a person wants first, then search across products, notes, similarities, availability and price context.

I want a winter fragrance with vanilla and amber, but not something overly sweet.
Show me fragrances similar in character to this one, available in Türkiye at a lower price.
I need something suitable for daily office use with good longevity.
Compare these two fragrances and explain which profile fits my preferences better.
AI-native architecture

Reasoning across fragrance data.

The product roadmap is designed around conversational discovery and model-assisted data intelligence rather than adding a chatbot on top of a conventional catalog.

  • Conversational fragrance search
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Semantic perfume and note comparison
  • Product similarity analysis
  • Catalog normalization and enrichment
  • Data-quality and inconsistency detection
  • Agentic research for catalog maintenance
